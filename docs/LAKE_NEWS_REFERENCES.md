# News and court references for the lakes

For each lake: what newspapers and courts have reported, followed by what AquaRevive's
satellite record shows. The links were checked on 28 Oct 2026; each one opened and was
about that lake. Dates are publication dates.

AquaRevive status terms: *persistent degradation* means below its own seasonal normal
for several years running. *Normal (too small)* means the lake is too small to rank
reliably. *Events* are changes the rain cannot explain (see the Change events tab).

## City-wide context

1. **No more construction within 75 metres of any lake in Bengaluru.** Citizen Matters, 4 May 2016. The NGT's Forward Foundation / Mantri Techzone judgment raised the lake buffer from 30 m to 75 m. https://citizenmatters.in/bellandur-lake-ngt-final-judgment-on-mantri-sez-bangalore/
2. **SC sets aside NGT order enlarging buffer zones around Bengaluru water bodies.** LiveLaw, 6 Mar 2019. The Supreme Court struck down the city-wide 75 m buffer, keeping it only for the two builders in the case. Our 0–30 m ring is the legal buffer (KTCDA); the 30–75 m ring is the earlier NGT standard. https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/sc-sets-aside-ngt-order-enlarging-buffer-zones-around-bengaluru-water-bodies-143346
3. **Bengaluru has lost more than 10,000 acres of water bodies to encroachments.** The News Minute, 23 Nov 2017. The Koliwad House Committee found 10,785 acres (about 18%) encroached across 1,547 lakes. https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/bengaluru-has-lost-more-10000-acres-water-bodies-encroachments-72052
4. **Citizens Action Group v. State of Karnataka** (W.P. 38401/2014, Karnataka High Court). This is the continuing lake-protection PIL: the court ordered 183 lakes kept free of sewage and, in 2022, evictions at Subramanyapura and Begur lakes. https://clpr.org.in/litigation/citizens-action-group-vs-state-of-karnataka/
